Day 3
🥾 Seven Lakes Valley — Slovenia's Greatest Day Hike
Wed Jun 10 · Bohinjska Bistrica
▼

The Seven Lakes Valley (Dolina Triglavskih Jezer) is the signature day hike of the Julian Alps — seven glacial lakes in a dramatic high-alpine cirque. One of the finest days possible in the mountains of Central Europe. 📍 AllTrails map

Distance~20 km / 12 mi
Elev. Gain1,500 m / 4,920 ft
Time8–10 hrs
DifficultyStrenuous
StartUkanc / Savica (taxi from Bistrica)
⏰ Start early — leave by 07:00 to avoid afternoon thunderstorms common above treeline in June.
  • Route: Savica Falls → steep forest trail → first lake (Črno jezero) → traverse all 7 lakes → optional extension to Hribarice plateau (2,358 m / 7,736 ft).
  • Mountain hut: Koča pri Triglavskih jezerih at the lakes serves hot meals. Budget for lunch here.
  • No technical climbing. Trail blazed with red-and-white markers throughout. Trekking poles strongly recommended.
  • Pack: 2+ litres of water per person, packed lunch, warm layers, rain gear. Conditions change fast above 1,800 m (5,900 ft).

✅ Recommended — Light Recovery Day (Old Town + Affenberg + Landskron)
  • Arrive 11:22 → walk into Villach old town. Hauptplatz, Stadtpfarrkirche tower (€3 for views), Drau riverside stroll. ~1–2 hrs.
  • Light lunch at a Gasthof — try Kasnudeln (Carinthian cheese-filled pasta, the regional specialty).
  • Taxi (~10 min, ~€15) to Affenberg Landskron — a semi-wild colony of ~150 Japanese macaques living free-range on a forested hillside. 1-hr guided walk; macaques approach within a metre or two. €13/adult, €8/teen. Tours typically 13:30, 14:30, 15:30, 16:30.
  • 5-min walk next door to Burgruine Landskron — castle ruins with panoramic Alpine views, restaurant with terrace, and a falconry show (typically 14:30 and 16:30). Coffee/cake at the castle, then taxi back for the 18:38 train.
  • Total taxi + admission cost ~€50–70 for the family. Genuinely memorable.
🏔️ Alternative — Dobratsch Summit Hike (only if everyone feels fresh)
  • Taxi from Villach to the Dobratsch Nature Park Aichingerhut starting point (~1,650 m / 5,410 ft). ~20–30 min, ~€20–25. ⚠️ Seasonal bus does NOT run on Thursdays — taxi only.
  • Hike to the Dobratsch summit (~2,166 m / 7,106 ft). Marked trail, no technical climbing. ~4 hrs round trip. Views across the Carinthian basin into Slovenia and Italy on clear days.
  • ⚠️ Reality check: after yesterday's Seven Lakes (~10 hrs, 1,500 m gain), legs may be cooked. Save energy for the Krn hike on Day 9. Default to the Recommended Day above.

Day 8
🥾 Soča Trail — Source to Bovec
Mon Jun 15 · Bovec
▼

The Soča River is one of Europe's most beautiful rivers — an impossible teal-green fed by glacial melt. The Soča Trail (Soška pot) follows it from source to sea; the upper section from the source to Bovec is the finest stretch.

Distance~20 km / 12 mi (one-way)
Elev. Gain~400 m / 1,310 ft
Time5–7 hrs
DifficultyModerate
StartIzvir Soče (source)
  • Taxi or morning bus to the Soča source (Izvir Soče, ~40 km / 25 miles from Bovec), then hike downstream back towards Bovec.
  • Highlights en route: Boka Waterfall (Slovenia's highest at 144 m / 472 ft — 20-min detour), Kozjak Waterfall (hidden in a grotto, 15-min side trail), Napoleon Bridge, Mlinarica Gorge.
  • Mostly flat — a great active-recovery day after the big climbs on Days 3 and 4. Shorter option: start from Trenta (~12 km / 7.5 miles).
  • The trail is well-marked and the scenery is consistently stunning all day. Don't rush — there's nowhere better to be.

🏔️ Lake Krn Hike via Lepena (Krnsko jezero, 1,394 m / 4,573 ft) · 📍 AllTrails

Krnsko jezero is the largest glacial lake in Slovenia, cradled in a high alpine basin beneath Mt Krn — a rewarding out-and-back that trades the strenuous summit push for a beautiful lake.

Distance11.7 km / 7.3 mi return
Elev. Gain885 m / 2,900 ft
Time5.5–6 hrs
DifficultyHard
StartLepena valley
  • Route: Lepena → mostly shaded forest climb on loose rock → easier valley walk to the lake. Dom dr. Klementa Juga mountain hut sits right at the lake — lunch and cheese from the summer shepherd. Swimming in the lake is not permitted.
  • Summit extension (strong legs only): from the lake, Mt Krn summit (2,244 m / 7,362 ft) adds ~3 hrs and ~860 m / 2,820 ft of climbing, turning it into a full 9–10 hr day. Decide at the lake based on energy and weather.
  • WWI context: The Isonzo Front (1915–1917) ran through here. Fortifications and cave shelters survive across the Krn massif. The Kobarid Museum covers this brilliantly — plan to visit it tomorrow on the way to Ljubljana.

🅐 Option A — Zagreb Day Trip (Croatia)
🚂 Not booked — €9 second-class, buy at Ljubljana station counter on the day. Direct train, ~2.5 hrs.
  • Take the 06:50 or 08:10 departure from Ljubljana (arrive Zagreb ~09:50 / ~10:40). Croatia is in the Schengen Area — no border formalities.
  • Return options: direct trains run 08:40, 10:34, 12:50, then nothing until 19:39 (arrive Ljubljana ~21:50). A full day means the 19:39 — only ~5 hrs before the 03:30 taxi. Factor that in.

📍 Zagreb Highlights

  • Dolac Market — rooftop farmers market above Ban Jelačić Square. Fresh produce, local honey. Closes early afternoon — go first.
  • Gornji Grad (Upper Town): Take the Uspinjača funicular (world's shortest, 66 m / 217 ft, €0.66). Kamenita Vrata shrine and St Mark's Church with its famous tiled roof.
  • Museum of Broken Relationships — genuinely moving and very popular with teenagers.
  • Zrinjevac Park & Lower Town: Art Nouveau arcades. Good for a coffee break.
  • Lunch: Tkalčičeva Street — try štrukli (baked cheese pastry) or a Zagreb schnitzel.
🅑 Option B — Relaxed Last Day in Ljubljana (recommended)
  • Sleep in and do a final laundry at the apartment (washer available). Repack at an easy pace before the 03:30 airport start.
  • Wander the old town at a stroll: ride the funicular up to Ljubljana Castle, follow the Ljubljanica riverside and its cafés, browse the Central Market, relax in Tivoli Park.
  • A short boat trip on the river is a lovely low-effort outing.
  • Best choice if everyone's worn out after 11 days of hiking — a calm wind-down with no late-night train to chase before the pre-dawn flight.

🌤️ Weather in June

Generally excellent — long days (sunset after 20:30), 20–25 °C (68–77 °F) in valleys, 10–15 °C (50–59 °F) at altitude.

⛈️ Afternoon thunderstorms are common above treeline. Start big hikes by 07:00 and aim to be below treeline by 14:00. Check arso.gov.si for mountain forecasts.
