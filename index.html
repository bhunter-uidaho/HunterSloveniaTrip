Day 3
🥾 Seven Lakes Valley — Slovenia's Greatest Day Hike
Wed Jun 10 · Bohinj
▼

The Seven Lakes Valley (Dolina Triglavskih Jezer) is the signature day hike of the Julian Alps — seven glacial lakes in a dramatic high-alpine cirque.

Distance~20 km
Elevation Gain1,500 m
Time8–10 hrs
DifficultyStrenuous
StartUkanc / Savica
⏰ Start early — leave by 07:00 to avoid afternoon thunderstorms common above treeline in June.

Route

  • Savica Falls → steep forest trail → first lake (Črno jezero)
  • Traverse all 7 lakes
  • Optional extension to Hribarice plateau (2,358 m)
🍽️ Mountain hut: Koča pri Triglavskih jezerih at the lakes serves hot meals. Worth a lunch stop.

No technical climbing. Trail blazed with red-and-white markers throughout. Trekking poles recommended.

Pack: 2+ litres of water per person, lunch, layers, rain gear.

Day 4
🇦🇹 Austria Day — Villach & Dobratsch
Thu Jun 11 · (Bohinj base)
▼

A rest day from hard hiking, but still active — and genuinely in Austria. The Karawanken range separates Slovenia from Carinthia; the tunnel train crossing is an experience in itself.

Bus: Bohinjska Bistrica → Jesenice (~35 min) · Train: Jesenice → Villach (~40 min) through the Karawanken Tunnel. Total one-way: ~1 hr 15 min. No border checks (Schengen).

Option A — Dobratsch Nature Park Summit Hike

Taxi or seasonal bus from Villach to the Aichingerhut starting point (~1,650 m), then hike to the Dobratsch summit (2,166 m, ~1.5 hrs, 516 m gain). Panoramic views into Slovenia, Italy, and Austria. Historic chapel at the top from 1692. Hiking info →

Option B — Villach Town + Light Walk

Villach old town (Hauptplatz, Stadtpfarrkirche tower for views), lunch at a Carinthian restaurant (try Kasnudeln — cheese-filled pasta), then an easy lakeside walk at Faaker See (20 min by bus).

Return: train Villach → Jesenice (~40 min), then bus back to Bohinj. Arrive by 19:00–20:00.

🏛️ Škocjan Caves — UNESCO World Heritage Site

Train Ljubljana → Divača (50 min), then taxi/shuttle to caves (~4 km). 2-hr guided tour through a cathedral-sized underground canyon — one of the largest in the world.

~€22 adult / €14 child. Book tickets in advance at park-skocjanske-jame.si

After tour: bus or taxi Divača → Piran (~45 min). Arrive early evening.

Explore Piran: Tartinijev Trg, St George's Church tower, the sea walls at sunset.

Day 8
🥾 Soča Trail — Source to Bovec
Mon Jun 15 · Bovec
▼

The Soča River is one of Europe's most beautiful rivers — an impossible teal-green fed by glacial melt. The Soča Trail (Soška pot) follows it from source to sea; the upper section is the highlight.

Distance~20 km one-way
Elevation Gain~400 m
Time5–7 hrs
DifficultyModerate
StartIzvir Soče (source)
Taxi or morning bus to the Soča source (Izvir Soče, ~40 km from Bovec), then hike downstream.

Highlights Along the Trail

  • Boka Waterfall — Slovenia's highest at 144 m (20-min detour)
  • Kozjak Waterfall — hidden in a grotto (15-min side trail)
  • Napoleon Bridge
  • Mlinarica Gorge

Mostly flat — a great active-recovery day after Seven Lakes. Shorter option: start from Trenta (~12 km).

Day 9
🥾 Mount Krn & Lake Krn (2,244 m)
Tue Jun 16 · Kobarid
▼

Move to Kobarid by bus (30 min), then tackle Mount Krn — one of the Julian Alps' finest summits, with the approach passing the largest glacial lake in Slovenia.

Distance~16 km return
Elevation Gain1,610 m
Time9–10 hrs
DifficultyVery Strenuous
StartLepena valley
Taxi from Kobarid to Lepena valley trailhead (~15 min, ~€15–20 each way). Ask your Airbnb host for a local driver.

Route

  • Lepena → forest trail → Lake Krn (Krnsko jezero) at 1,394 m (3 hrs, 750 m gain)
  • → Krn summit (2,244 m), 3 more hrs, 860 m more
  • Mountain hut at the lake for lunch

Shorter option: Lake Krn only (6–7 hrs, 750 m gain) if energy is flagging.

⚔️ WWI context: The Isonzo Front (1915–1917) ran through here. Fortifications and cave shelters survive on the summit. The Kobarid Museum in town covers this brilliantly — well worth an hour the evening before.
Day 10
Kobarid Historical Loop + Bohinj Railway to Ljubljana
Wed Jun 17 · Ljubljana
▼

Morning: Kobarid Historical Walk (Zgodovinska pot)

Distance5 km loop
Elevation Gain200 m
Time~2 hrs
DifficultyEasy–Moderate

From town to the Soča gorge, past waterfalls, Italian fortifications, Napoleon Bridge, and WWI trenches. Suitable for all fitness levels. Pairs well with the Kobarid Museum (1 hr, fascinating for teens).

🚂 Midday: Board the scenic Bohinj Railway from Most na Soči → Bohinjska Bistrica (through a 6-km tunnel beneath the Julian Alps) → Jesenice → Ljubljana. One of Slovenia's great railway journeys.

Arrive Ljubljana: late afternoon. Easy evening in the city.

Day 11
🇭🇷 Day Trip: Zagreb, Croatia
Thu Jun 18 · Ljubljana
▼

Zagreb is one of Central Europe's most underrated capitals — compact, medieval upper town, excellent open-air market, great coffee culture. Croatia is in the Schengen Area and uses the euro, so no border hassles.

Train Ljubljana → Zagreb: ~2.5 hrs direct. Take the 07:00–08:00 departure.
Return: 17:00 or 18:00 train from Zagreb, arriving Ljubljana by 20:00–20:30.

Suggested Day in Zagreb

  • Dolac Market — Rooftop open-air farmers market. Fresh produce, local honey. Closes early afternoon — go first.
  • Gornji Grad (Upper Town) — Take the Uspinjača funicular (world's shortest, 66 m, €0.66). Visit Kamenita Vrata and St Mark's Church with its famous tiled roof.
  • Museum of Broken Relationships — Objects donated by people worldwide, each with the story of a relationship that ended. Genuinely moving and very popular with teenagers.
  • Zrinjevac Park & Lower Town — Art Nouveau arcades. Good for a coffee break.
  • Lunch: Tkalčičeva Street area. Try štrukli (baked cheese pastry) or a Zagreb schnitzel.
⚠️ Head to the station by 17:00–18:00. 06:00 flight tomorrow means a 03:30 taxi. Be home from Zagreb by 20:30, have a farewell dinner, pack, and sleep.

🚂 Getting Around — Key Journey Legs

Day 2: Ljubljana → Bohinj

Train: Ljubljana → Jesenice → Bohinjska Bistrica (~2 hrs). Bus or taxi to Lake Bohinj from Bohinjska Bistrica (~20 min). Stay near Ukanc for the Seven Lakes trailhead.

Day 5: Bohinj → Piran (via Škocjan Caves)

Train Bohinjska Bistrica → Ljubljana (~2 hrs via Jesenice). Train stop at Divača, 4 km from the caves. After tour: bus Divača → Piran (~45 min). Total: ~3.5–4 hrs.

Day 7: Piran → Bovec

Bus Piran → Koper (~30 min) → Nova Gorica (~1.5 hrs) → Bovec (~1.5–2 hrs). Total: ~3.5–4 hrs.

Day 9: Bovec → Kobarid

Bus Bovec → Kobarid: 30–40 min, several buses daily.

Day 10: Kobarid → Ljubljana (Scenic Bohinj Railway)

Bus Kobarid → Tolmin → Most na Soči. Then board the scenic Bohinj Railway: Most na Soči → Bohinjska Bistrica (through a 6-km tunnel beneath the Julian Alps) → Jesenice → Ljubljana. One of Slovenia's great railway journeys.

Day 11: Ljubljana → Zagreb, Croatia

Direct train Ljubljana → Zagreb: ~2.5 hrs. Take the 07:00–08:00 departure for a full day. Return trains around 17:00–18:00, arriving Ljubljana by 20:00–20:30.

🌤️ Weather in June

Generally excellent — long days (sunset after 20:30), 20–25 °C in valleys, 10–15 °C at altitude.

⛈️ Afternoon thunderstorms are common above treeline. Start big hikes by 07:00 and aim to be below treeline by 14:00. Check arso.si for mountain forecasts.

💡 Bonus Options

Kranjska Gora add-on

If you want to add the Prisojnik / Okno via ferrata (C/D difficulty, 2,547 m) or the Mount Peč Triple Border hike (where Slovenia, Austria, and Italy meet), add 2 nights Kranjska Gora after Bohinj. Prisojnik requires prior via ferrata experience — hire a local guide.

Alternative to Zagreb (Day 11)

The Kamnik Alps (Kamniško Sedlo, 2,000 m, 12 km) make a great final hike; bus from Ljubljana to Kamnik is 40 min.
