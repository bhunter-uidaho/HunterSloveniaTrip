Day 3
🥾 Seven Lakes Valley — Slovenia's Greatest Day Hike
Wed Jun 10 · Bohinjska Bistrica
▼

The Seven Lakes Valley (Dolina Triglavskih Jezer) is the signature day hike of the Julian Alps — seven glacial lakes in a dramatic high-alpine cirque. One of the finest days possible in the mountains of Central Europe.

Distance~20 km / 12 mi
Elev. Gain1,500 m / 4,920 ft
Time8–10 hrs
DifficultyStrenuous
StartUkanc / Savica (taxi from Bistrica)
⏰ Start early — leave by 07:00 to avoid afternoon thunderstorms common above treeline in June.
  • Route: Savica Falls → steep forest trail → first lake (Črno jezero) → traverse all 7 lakes → optional extension to Hribarice plateau (2,358 m / 7,736 ft).
  • Mountain hut: Koča pri Triglavskih jezerih at the lakes serves hot meals. Budget for lunch here.
  • No technical climbing. Trail blazed with red-and-white markers throughout. Trekking poles strongly recommended.
  • Pack: 2+ litres of water per person, packed lunch, warm layers, rain gear. Conditions change fast above 1,800 m (5,900 ft).

🏔️ Option A — Dobratsch Nature Park Summit Hike
  • Taxi from Villach to the Aichingerhut starting point (~1,650 m / 5,410 ft). ~20–30 min, ~€20–25.

  • Short walks around the city park, or easy lakeside walk at Faaker See (20 min by bus from Villach). A gentle recovery before tomorrow's early start.

Day 8
🥾 Soča Trail — Source to Bovec
Mon Jun 15 · Bovec
▼

The Soča River is one of Europe's most beautiful rivers — an impossible teal-green fed by glacial melt. The Soča Trail (Soška pot) follows it from source to sea; the upper section from the source to Bovec is the finest stretch.

Distance~20 km / 12 mi (one-way)
Elev. Gain~400 m / 1,310 ft
Time5–7 hrs
DifficultyModerate
StartIzvir Soče (source)
  • Taxi or morning bus to the Soča source (Izvir Soče, ~40 km / 25 miles from Bovec), then hike downstream back towards Bovec.
  • Highlights en route: Boka Waterfall (Slovenia's highest at 144 m / 472 ft — 20-min detour), Kozjak Waterfall (hidden in a grotto, 15-min side trail), Napoleon Bridge, Mlinarica Gorge.
  • Mostly flat — a great active-recovery day after the big climbs on Days 3 and 4. Shorter option: start from Trenta (~12 km / 7.5 miles).
  • The trail is well-marked and the scenery is consistently stunning all day. Don't rush — there's nowhere better to be.

🏔️ Mount Krn Hike (2,244 m / 7,362 ft)

One of the Julian Alps' finest summits, with the approach passing Krnsko jezero — the largest glacial lake in Slovenia.

Distance~16 km / 10 mi return
Elev. Gain1,610 m / 5,280 ft
Time9–10 hrs
DifficultyVery Strenuous
StartLepena valley
  • Taxi from Kobarid to Lepena valley trailhead: ~15 min, ~€15–20 each way. Ask your Airbnb host for a recommended local driver.
  • Route: Lepena → forest trail → Lake Krn (Krnsko jezero) at 1,394 m / 4,573 ft (3 hrs, 750 m / 2,460 ft gain) → Krn summit, 3 more hrs, 860 m / 2,820 ft more. Mountain hut at the lake for lunch.
  • Shorter option: Lake Krn only (6–7 hrs total, 750 m gain) if energy is flagging after the Soča Trail day.
  • WWI context: The Isonzo Front (1915–1917) ran through here. Fortifications and cave shelters survive on the Krn summit. The Kobarid Museum covers this brilliantly — consider visiting in the evening.

Day 11
🇭🇷 Day Trip: Zagreb, Croatia
Thu Jun 18 · Ljubljana
▼

Zagreb is one of Central Europe's most underrated capitals — compact, medieval upper town, excellent open-air market, great coffee culture. Croatia is in the Schengen Area so no border formalities.

🚂 Getting There ⚠️ Buy tickets

Not booked — buy at Ljubljana station or potniski.sz.si. Direct train, ~2.5 hrs.

  • Take the 07:00–08:00 departure from Ljubljana for a full day.
  • Return: 17:00 or 18:00 train from Zagreb → arrive Ljubljana 20:00–20:30.
  • ⚠️ 06:00 flight tomorrow means a 03:30 taxi. Be back in Ljubljana by 20:30 at the absolute latest.
📍 Zagreb Highlights
  • Dolac Market — rooftop open-air farmers market above Ban Jelačić Square. Fresh produce, local honey. Closes early afternoon — go first.
  • Gornji Grad (Upper Town): Take the Uspinjača funicular (world's shortest, 66 m / 217 ft, €0.66). Visit Kamenita Vrata (Stone Gate shrine) and St Mark's Church with its famous tiled roof.
  • Museum of Broken Relationships — objects donated by people around the world, each with the story of a relationship that ended. Genuinely moving and very popular with teenagers.
  • Zrinjevac Park & Lower Town: Art Nouveau arcades and parks. Good for a coffee break.
  • Lunch: Tkalčičeva Street area. Try štrukli (baked cheese pastry) or a Zagreb schnitzel.
  • Head to the station by 17:00–18:00 for the return train.

🌤️ Weather in June

Generally excellent — long days (sunset after 20:30), 20–25 °C (68–77 °F) in valleys, 10–15 °C (50–59 °F) at altitude.

⛈️ Afternoon thunderstorms are common above treeline. Start big hikes by 07:00 and aim to be below treeline by 14:00. Check arso.gov.si for mountain forecasts.
